Management reported that **Aehr Test Systems, Inc.** ( AEHR +19.82% ) completed a transition year, shifting its primary revenue base from electric vehicle silicon carbide applications to AI and data center infrastructure. The company provided fiscal 2027 revenue guidance of $130 million to $150 million, supported by a record effective backlog of over $100 million. Strategic focus has moved toward wafer-level burn-in for AI accelerators and silicon photonics, which together accounted for the majority of fourth quarter revenue. The company is scaling its manufacturing capacity through partnerships in Southeast Asia and facility upgrades in Fremont to support anticipated volume ramps for its FOX and Sonoma platforms.
