[Tim Archer — CEO, prepared remarks]
Record revenue, operating margin, and EPS in the June quarter. Sequential growth led by a DOUBLING of NAND revenue from the prior quarter — "the growing importance of storage to AI system performance." CSBG posted strong growth on upgrades, Reliant, and Equipment Intelligence-enabled services.

CY2026 WFE now expected in the LOW $150 BILLION range, up from the prior $140B outlook (the prior "upside bias" played out). September-quarter revenue guide of $8.1B = >20% growth QoQ; 2026 shaping up as the THIRD consecutive year of outperformance vs WFE.

2027: "an EXTRAORDINARY SETUP for WFE growth." Customers report unprecedented long-term demand visibility, announced multi-year new-fab timelines, and are working with Lam to secure equipment orders to fill incremental clean-room space as it comes online. AI progressing in waves — training → inference → agentic → physical AI. NAND: expanding context windows and persistent memory driving significantly higher flash demand; near term customers add bits via installed-base conversions to 200+ layer architectures. Lam SAM PER WAFER IN NAND TO DOUBLE from the 128-layer node to 500+ layer devices.

Foundry/logic + DRAM: GAA, CFET, HBM, 4F-squared, panel-level advanced packaging all feature in AI roadmaps; deposition/etch intensity the common thread. Moving toward HIGH-30s SAM as % of WFE FASTER than outlined at the 2025 Investor Day.

Product highlights: Conductor etch installed base >40,000 chambers. AKARA (direct-drive plasma; high-aspect-ratio patterning): first adopted for 2nm-and-below GAA, now gaining in advanced DRAM — several strategic tool-of-record positions including recent wins for the MOST CHALLENGING GATE ETCH applications; Akara installed base has DOUBLED EVERY YEAR since launch, expected again in 2027. DRAM adopting VECTOR hard-mask deposition for low-K patterning (co-optimized with conductor etch; >20% cost savings vs traditional approaches) and VECTOR diffusion-barrier systems (interface clean + etch-stop + hermetic protection; ~5% capacitance reduction vs competing technologies). ARGOS selective etch: surface-treatment applications roughly DOUBLE in FinFET→GAA transition; winning leading-edge foundry logic at 2nm-and-below, expanding into DRAM.

Advanced packaging: industry leader in TSV etch + electroplating ("drill and fill"), on track for GREATER THAN 70% year-on-year growth. Future AI packages exceeding 9x RETICLE SIZE (~3x larger than today's mainstream) → industry moving beyond wafer-based toward PANEL-LEVEL packaging. Lam has shipped 510mm x 515mm PANEL SYSTEMS into development programs across multiple geographies; will ship the first 310mm x 310mm panel tool THIS YEAR.

CSBG: DEXTRO COBOTS (industry-first collaborative maintenance robots) seeing rapid adoption; automated-maintenance PM task coverage has DOUBLED since the start of 2026; NAND-proven Equipment Intelligence/Dextro solutions now expanding into DRAM (additional service revenue H2); very early stages of a multi-year rollout.

[Doug Bettinger — CFO, prepared remarks]
Fourth consecutive quarter of record revenue; HIGHEST QUARTERLY GROSS MARGIN IN 20 YEARS; record operating margin and EPS. FY2026: record revenue $23.2B, GM 50.6%, diluted EPS $5.82 (+41% vs FY2025) — ahead of Investor Day profitability objectives.

June quarter: revenue $6.72B (+15% QoQ, +30% YoY), above midpoint; GM 52.0% (above high end; pricing actions + operational/scale efficiencies + mix); opex $916M (67% R&D); op margin 38.4% (above high end); tax 11% (rising to MID-TEENS from September quarter — US GILTI + jurisdiction mix — and staying up); EPS $1.82 record on ~1.26B shares. Deferred revenue $2.43B, +$213M QoQ — largest driver CUSTOMER DOWN PAYMENTS.

Segment mix (systems revenue): memory 46% (record dollars; up from 39%) — NVM/NAND 23% (up from 12%; NAND DOLLARS MORE THAN DOUBLED QoQ on 256+ layer-class conversions, primarily ENTERPRISE SSDs); DRAM 23% (flattish dollars at the March-quarter record; 1-alpha/1-beta/1-gamma wafer adds + upgrades enabling DDR5/LPDDR5/HBM). Foundry 44% (from 54%; China mature-node down, offset by leading-edge 2nm/3nm + advanced packaging strength). Logic/other 10%.

Region: Taiwan 27% (record dollars), China 26% (from 34% — multinationals in China GREW, domestic declined; expect similar trend rest of year), Korea 20%. CSBG: third consecutive record, nearly $2.5B, +17% QoQ, +43% YoY; record upgrade revenue; spares strong on high utilization.

Cash $5.6B; DSO 72 days; inventory $4.3B (turns 3.0x, best in ~5 years); capex $189M; headcount +1,800 QoQ to ~22,400 (factory/field + R&D). Capital return: $246M buyback + $325M dividends = 45% of FCF in Q; 81% YTD; ≥85% over time; $4B authorization remaining.

September-quarter guidance: revenue $8.1B ± $400M; GM 52% ± 1pt; op margin 39.5% ± 1pt; EPS $2.15 ± $0.15 on ~1.255B shares. Spending grows much slower than revenue.

LONG-TERM MODEL RAISED vs Investor Day: SAM expansion trending to the high-30s% of WFE; intend to drive GROSS MARGIN TO MID-50s% and OPERATING MARGIN TO MID-40s% over the next several years.
