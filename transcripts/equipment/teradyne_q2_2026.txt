[Greg Smith — CEO]
Second straight record quarter: revenue >$1.3B, +>100% YoY; non-GAAP EPS $2.47, +>300%. AI-DRIVEN REVENUE >60% OF TOTAL — proof the wafer-to-AI-data-center strategy is working. All three groups (Semi Test, Product Test, Robotics) grew YoY and QoQ.

THE ATE-TAM THESIS (new frame): WFE capex forecast to approach $250 BILLION by end of decade -> 5-10%/yr 300mm wafer growth and 15-20% CAGR in TOTAL TRANSISTOR PRODUCTION (first-order predictor of the SoC test TAM; memory BIT growth, also 15-20% CAGR, predicts the memory TAM). Second driver: ADVANCED PACKAGING — more chiplets/memories/CPO per package raises test intensity per die (latent-defect economics). After a decade of semi-cap outgrowing test, THE TREND REVERSED IN 2024 — test is now OUTPACING fab equipment. Path for TOTAL ATE TAM TO REACH OR EXCEED $20 BILLION by end of decade. Test as % of semi capex: ~4% (2023) -> 7% (2025) -> 8% (first 5 months of 2026); expected to SETTLE AT 7-9%.

Share: first MERCHANT GPU order (received Q1) DELIVERED in Q2; in Q3, CORRELATION COMPLETED AT A SECOND HYPERSCALER -> confidence in 2027 share growth. DUAL-VENDOR framework at large compute customers (de-risking supply): 4 phases — opportunity -> working solution -> correlation -> production ramp (9-12 months); then a FAST-FOLLOWER phase converting additional parts, with share going 0% -> ~30%; years to reach MATURE dual-vendor (either platform releases new parts). Current status: ONE compute customer MATURE dual-vendor, ONE in FAST FOLLOWER, ONE IN QUALIFICATION pre-ramp. Memory: 2026 memory TAM likely +>40% vs 2025; HBM-based die test a highlight (Magnum's logic-test capability = swing-tool advantage). Compute is H1-weighted; next surge H1 2027.

Networking: extreme growth across scale-out/across/up; timing of cable->backplane and pluggable->XPO/CPO transitions in flux, but 'robust growth in ALL of these technologies' — hence Quantifi Photonics acquisition, PHOTON 100 (optical test), MLTP JV (multi-lane copper). CPO ALONE = $300-700M TEST TAM BY 2028. IST +2.5x QoQ on AI HDD demand (>20%/yr exabyte growth). Data-center build-out also drives Product Test + Robotics (multibillion-dollar CM/ODM automation+test TAM, mid-double-digit growth). 2027: 'another year of healthy growth'; target model update at the Q4 call.

[Michelle Turner — CFO]
Revenue and EPS above high end. H1-2026: $2.6B revenue (+~100%), $5.02 EPS (+275%). Semi Test >$1B again: SoC $843M, MEMORY $212M (3rd straight >$200M record; BOOK-TO-BILL OVER 2), IST $67M (+94%; all 3 HDD suppliers). COMPUTE = 70% of SoC revenue, +~600% YoY. Auto/industrial strengthening on POWER MANAGEMENT for AI data centers. Product Test $107M (+26%; Omnics board-test platform shipping; MLTP momentum). Robotics $100M (+33%; electronics-mfg/semi now the LARGEST segment; US manufacturing center opens later this year; US = 32% of robotics sales). GM 59.8% (+250bps YoY). Opinc $448M (33.7%). FCF $378M (H1 $579M, +150%). Cash $517M.

Q3 guide: $1.2-1.3B revenue; EPS $1.85-2.15; GM 58-59% (mix + new-product launches); opex 29-30% of sales (R&D + go-to-market for 2027); H1 = 50-52% of the year. H2 growth in memory, auto/industrial, IST, Product Test, Robotics — offset by mobile softness and COMPUTE ORDER TIMING (next compute surge H1-2027). 'We anticipate a RESURGENCE IN GROWTH IN 2027 driven by ATE TAM expansion AND market share gains.' Memory a gross-margin strain into 2027.
