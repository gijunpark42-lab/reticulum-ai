CEO (Gaby Waisman): Nova delivered another record quarter, exceeding guidance across financial and operational metrics. Q1 revenue $235.3M with record profitability — record revenue from memory devices on robust advanced-DRAM demand plus strong momentum in advanced packaging and gate-all-around. Multiple customer wins and record sales in several product lines.

Gartner market-share report: +400bps share gain in film & critical-dimension metrology — second consecutive year of significant share growth — Nova now the market's SECOND-LARGEST vendor.

AI remains the fundamental driver, with the shift toward AGENTIC AI models increasing computing and memory requirements — accelerating capacity expansion across Logic, Memory, and advanced packaging while raising manufacturing complexity and yield challenges → higher process-control/metrology intensity.

Capacity expansion + tighter customer planning alignment improving visibility. New manufacturing facility under construction in ASIA — operational toward END of 2026 (capacity, cost structure, load balancing, closer to customers/supply chain).

Memory: record revenue; advanced DRAM ~2/3 of memory business. Record Metrion (in-line SIMS) sales incl. repeat purchases by a leading memory customer for advanced 3D NAND AND DRAM (moving from initial adoption to broader deployment). Nova AncoScene (front-end chemical metrology) record sales + additional share gained at a leading memory customer in Asia (multiple tool deliveries through the year). XPS and dimensional metrology supported by capacity expansions. Robust HBM-RELATED BOOKINGS for Nova WMC and Semdex standalone platforms.

Logic: record integrated-metrology revenue driven by GAA and new customer penetrations in mature nodes and advanced packaging. Intel awarded Nova the INTEL EPIC SUPPLIER AWARD (highest honor in Intel's global supply chain; select list in 2026) — underscores scale/depth of engagement with Intel.

Advanced packaging: another record quarter. Nova WMC addresses increased WARPAGE and non-uniform surfaces from new packaging schemes. HYBRID BONDING adoption progressing FASTER THAN EXPECTED in several advanced device segments — direct Cu-Cu interfaces and ultrafine pitch are highly metrology-intensive (surface planarity, alignment accuracy, interface integrity across multiple steps). Strong positioning of optical and chemical platforms at top-tier manufacturers.

Services: record revenue, 13th consecutive quarter of sequential growth.

Customer schedule considerations (pull-ins) positively impact H1; H2 expected to run HIGHER.

CFO (Guy Kizner): Record revenue $235.3M (above high end; +6% QoQ, +10% YoY). Mix ~66% Logic/Foundry, 34% Memory. 4 customers and 5 regions each >10% of product revenue. GAAP GM 57.7% / non-GAAP 59.4% (upper end of 57-60% target). GAAP opex $64.9M / non-GAAP $59.4M. Operating margin 30% GAAP / 34% non-GAAP (above 28-33% target). Tax ~17%. GAAP EPS $2.04 / non-GAAP $2.33 (above high end).

Q2 2026 guidance: revenue $245-255M; GAAP EPS $2.10-2.24; non-GAAP EPS $2.34-2.48; GM ~57% GAAP / ~59% non-GAAP; non-GAAP opex ~$66M; tax ~15%.
