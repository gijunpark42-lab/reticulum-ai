Entegris ( ENTG +1.24% ) reported fiscal first-quarter results featuring year-over-year revenue growth, expanded margins, and elevated free cash flow, with key financial metrics surpassing guidance. Management highlighted long-term structural improvement initiatives, including manufacturing optimization, deleveraging, and growing content per wafer, particularly in advanced logic and memory supported by AI demand. The company provided specific guidance pointing to continued top-line and margin improvements in upcoming quarters, driven by anticipated increases in wafer fab equipment, fab construction, and incremental CapEx revenue, as well as expanding opportunities in advanced packaging.
