Added ANOTHER GIGAWATT of capacity this quarter; on track to ROUGHLY DOUBLE overall capacity in two years. Copilot workload throughput 4x since the start of the year. Sovereignty: Mistral partnership for Microsoft Sovereign Cloud (public, customer-controlled, fully disconnected). Silicon: MAIA 200 scaling — 30% BETTER PERFORMANCE PER DOLLAR than the latest-generation hardware in the fleet, now supporting BOTH OpenAI AND MAI models; 'we will be AMONG THE FIRST cloud providers to deploy next-generation rack-scale AI infrastructure based on AMD HELIOS and NVIDIA VERA RUBIN.' For agents, CPUs matter as much as GPUs: COBALT VMs power first-party workloads plus Adobe, Arm, Elastic, OpenAI, Sprinklr, TomTom; COBALT 200 racks in OVER 25 DATA CENTERS by month-end.

Platform: broadest model catalog — OVER 11,000 MODELS (OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral, xAI, MAI family); 5x increase in customers building with multiple providers. MAI: a dozen+ new models (image, voice, transcription, coding, security) incl. first reasoning model MAI THINKING-1; co-designed with silicon — 40% better performance per WATT running MAI on Maia 200. Model-substitutable HARNESS architecture (harness, context, memory, action space separate from any model family): MAI-Code-1-Flash on GitHub Copilot (higher acceptance, 10% lower median token usage); in Excel comparable to GPT-5.6 at much lower cost; MAI-CYBER-1-FLASH outperforms 'much larger MYTHOS model' at HALF the cost in the multi-agent security harness (90% of tasks on Flash, 10% to frontier); 89% GPU-cost reduction in Dynamics (MAI-Voice-2-Flash), 84% in PowerPoint (MAI-Image-2.5). FOUNDRY: 100,000 customers, revenue MORE THAN DOUBLED YoY; customers at 1T-token annualized run rate 4x YoY. AGENT 365: ~40 MILLION AGENTS registered across tens of thousands of companies, two months in.

Apps: M365 COPILOT OVER 30 MILLION PAID SEATS, net adds MORE THAN DOUBLED QoQ; conversations/user ~2x YoY; weekly engagement on par with Outlook/Teams; deployment-to-high-usage (80% MAU) fell from months to DAYS; >50,000-seat customers up 7x YoY; majority-of-workforce deployments +75% QoQ. NHS England 505,000 seats (largest healthcare; saved 43 min/day in trial); KPMG 276,000; HSBC 200,000; AstraZeneca/Boeing/Infosys/P&G/Stellantis/TCS/UPMC/Wells Fargo/Wipro 60,000+ each. GITHUB: Copilot 50M users; GitHub 225M users; 1 IN 3 PULL REQUESTS involve an agent; Copilot revenue ACCELERATED >60% QoQ after June usage-based pricing. Healthcare: 100M automated patient encounters pace (28M this quarter, 2x). MICROSOFT FRONTIER COMPANY launched: 6,000 embedded industry/engineering experts (330 projects, 164 customers in the pilot year).
