[Andy Jassy — CEO]
Revenue $200.6B (+20%); operating income $27.5B (+43%). AWS 'is booming': +36.7% YoY — FIFTH STRAIGHT QUARTER OF ACCELERATION, fastest growth in 18 quarters (when AWS was less than half its size). Added OVER $4.6 BILLION in revenue QoQ — ~80% MORE than the largest sequential increase ever. BACKLOG $496 BILLION, GROWING TRIPLE DIGITS YoY. AWS = $169B annualized run rate (would rank ~24th on the Fortune 500 standalone). CHIPS business run rate >$25B, +triple-digit % YoY; AI revenue run rate also >$25B, +triple-digit % YoY.

Why: broadest capabilities; inference wants to live near apps + data (more resides on AWS than anywhere); strongest security/ops. AI growth DRIVES CORE growth: post-training reinforcement learning and AGENT TOOL USE run mostly on CPUs (not accelerators) — Graviton = strongest CPU (30-40% better price-performance), plus storage/vector databases. SageMaker (companies building own non-frontier models on proprietary data), Bedrock (best model selection + governance; growing incredibly fast), Bedrock Agents (managed agent infrastructure: policies, payments, web search, new harness, Strands). Turnkey agents: coding agents incl. Claude Code, Codex, and Amazon's spec-driven KIRO — up to 50% more cost-effective, USAGE TRIPLED QoQ. Amazon Q (AI work companion across email/calendar/files/SaaS incl. Slack, Salesforce, Jira, Teams, ServiceNow; autonomous background agents added): customers incl. 3M, Allianz, AstraZeneca, Autodesk, BMW, Exxon, FINRA, Hyundai, Intuit, Mondelez, Moody's, NBA, NFL, Sun Life, Southwest. NEW: AWS CONTINUUM — agentic security service that discovers/prioritizes/validates/remediates code vulnerabilities (frontier models are making vulnerabilities easier to find; enterprises all ask about security).

TRAINIUM: 'the two leading AI labs in the world, Anthropic and OpenAI,' have multi-year MULTI-GIGAWATT commitments; growing startup adoption — unicorns Neurorobotics and Odyssey joining Twelve Labs, Descartes Labs, Poolside AI, Karakuri, Metagenomi, NetoAI, Splash Music — plus Uber and Pinterest. Graviton used by 98% of top-1,000 EC2 customers; revenue commitments nearly 3x QoQ; GRAVITON5 RAMPING NEARLY 2x FASTER THAN GRAVITON4. Deep NVIDIA partnership continues — 'customers want choice.'

CAPEX AND ECONOMICS: 'We now believe we will spend approximately $220 BILLION IN CASH CAPEX IN 2026 — the HIGHER COST OF MEMORY pushing this number up from our prior estimate of about $200 billion.' Framework: DATA CENTERS — capital starts ~2 years before servers go in; 30+ year monetization life; 5-6 server generations per DC (later generations better economics). SERVERS/NETWORKING — bought a few months ahead with demand visibility ('if the demand isn't there, we won't spend'); <3-year average breakeven; 5-6+ year useful life; MOST AI CAPACITY CONTRACTED AT >=5-YEAR TERMS → significant FCF in years 2-3 post-breakeven. Near-term FCF headwinds while many DCs build simultaneously; 'at some point' revenue growth outpaces incremental capex → compelling FCF/ROIC (same pattern as cloud 1.0, and AI margins are tracking core's evolution 'actually a little ahead'). EVEN AT $220B, NOT ENOUGH CAPACITY FOR 2026 DEMAND — 'this dynamic will also be true in 2027, too. The demand we already have for 2028 is STRIKING.' AWS long-term: previously believed a few-hundred-billion-dollar business — now 'AT LEAST DOUBLE that, and very possibly a TRILLION-DOLLAR ANNUAL REVENUE business in time.'

Stores/ads highlights: ads $19.8B (+26%); Prime Day record; grocery #2 US grocer (>$150B GMS); same-day perishables in 2,300 US cities; Amazon Now 9 countries/250+ cities; robotics arm fleet (Cardinal/Sparrow) to MORE THAN DOUBLE in 2026; Alexa+ expanding (DE/AT/FR/BR); AMAZON LEO ~400 satellites in orbit — initial satellite internet service THIS YEAR, meaningful enterprise/government revenue commitments, 20+ partners.

[Brian Olsavsky — CFO]
Revenue $200.6B (+20% ex-FX; includes Prime Day shifting into Q2). Operating income $27.5B incl. ~$1.2B of benefits: ~$600M tariff-related refunds (North America) + ~$600M fair-value gain on ENERGY CONTRACTS under derivative accounting (mostly AWS; contracts secure electricity for existing/future operations). Segments: North America $116.2B (+16%), margin 7.9% ($9.1B); International $42.2B (+15% ex-FX), margin 4.1% ($1.7B); AWS $42.2B (+36.7%), operating income $16.6B. Paid units +17%. AWS margin drivers: 'disciplined efficiency gains, capacity optimization... custom silicon and custom network gear'; margins +650bps YoY (+520bps ex-derivative gain); 'these margins will fluctuate.' CASH CAPEX $53.1B in Q2 — primarily AWS and generative AI. Q3 guide: net sales $197-202B (Prime Day timing costs ~400bps of growth optically; FX -80bps); operating income $22.5-26.5B. Fuel/line-haul inflation from Middle East conflict partially offset by an FBA fuel/logistics surcharge (April). Memory/hard-drive/SSD price inflation acknowledged: existing contracts keep agreed prices for their duration; NEW deals price in current costs.

[Q&A highlights]
- Doug Anmuth (JPM): AI workloads assumed low-margin — why 39% AWS margin? Own frontier model: AWS can be wildly successful WITHOUT one (no one model rules; Bedrock has them all) — BUT Amazon IS pursuing its own frontier model for COST control, PRIORITIZATION, and SPEED: 'within the next few years you're going to have at least a half dozen models comparably good... one of them will be ours.'
