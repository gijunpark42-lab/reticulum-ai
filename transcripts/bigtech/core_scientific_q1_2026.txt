Management confirmed colocation revenue from 243 megawatts now covers operating costs and expands margins, representing over $350 million in annualized GAAP revenue. The company's billable capacity is positioned to surpass 450 megawatts by late summer and reach 590 megawatts by early 2027, as contractual execution with CoreWeave advances. Behind-the-meter infrastructure strategies—including gas pipelines and grid-connected solutions—are in development at Pecos and Muskogee to accelerate expansion and reduce supply risks. Labor, equipment, and development contracts have been proactively secured to maintain construction timelines and support growth, with $2 billion in capital expenditures planned for 2026.
