[Mark Zuckerberg — CEO]
3.6 BILLION people use at least one Meta app daily. Milestones: Instagram 2B DAU; Threads crossed 500M MAU (fastest-growing conversation app ever); Facebook >2B DAU; WhatsApp all-time messaging record — 30 MILLION MESSAGES/SECOND peak during the World Cup final. Kunal Shah joins as Head of WhatsApp. Three pillars: (1) AI accelerating every major part of the core business; (2) personal agents as the next wave of products; (3) a large ENTERPRISE opportunity — APIs, business agents, 'POTENTIALLY SELLING COMPUTE DIRECTLY,' and other services.

Core: LLMs integrated into recommendations (first-principles content understanding + user goals). Muse Image / Muse Video open a 'nearly infinite universe' of personalized content beyond friends and creators. Ads: LLM ranking with expanded context — 'on a dollar basis, our ads business is reporting FASTER year-over-year revenue growth than any other company's reported ad business.' 9M small businesses use at least one AI ad-creative tool. Meta One subscription launched (tiering to come). Shipping velocity: Instagram Instants, Forum (groups app), Seller (marketplace app).

Meta Superintelligence Labs (1 year in): shipped Muse Spark 1.1 (strong agentic coding model — computer use, tool use, multimodal; efficient) + Muse Image. Meta AI daily interactions +60% since the Muse Spark integration, growing week over week. Muse Spark available through the new PUBLIC API; enterprise-adoption features being built.

Agents: personal agents working 24/7 on your goals ('extremely unlikely that in five years billions of people DON'T have one') — needs to be consumer-simple, unlike today's fiddly proto-agents; WhatsApp = leading Meta AI surface; incognito mode launched (private even from Meta). BUSINESS AGENTS: Meta Business Agent global on WhatsApp/Messenger — MORE THAN 1 MILLION BUSINESSES using weekly; rolling to Instagram; roadmap: summaries, overnight digests, competitive intelligence, 'business-in-a-box.' Monetization: subscriptions, volume pricing, evolving toward pay-per-result and 'an efficient AUCTION OVER OUR COMPUTE, similar to advertisers today.'

Compute: NEW STRATEGIC VENTURE WITH BLACKROCK announced (yesterday, part of Meta Compute) to develop a 1 GW DATA CENTER IN EL PASO, TEXAS. 'We're getting A LOT OF OFFERS FOR COMPUTE AT A SIGNIFICANT PREMIUM over what we paid for it' — but 'there will continue to be a significantly higher margin on selling INTELLIGENCE rather than selling compute directly'; optimistic on meaningful growth across ALL the enterprise lines. Glasses: 'one of the fastest-growing consumer electronics of all time'; new Meta glasses line with EssilorLuxottica (incl. Kylie Jenner style), first to ship with Muse Spark out of the box; early sales exceeded expectations; Connect Sept 23.

[Susan Li — CFO]
Family of Apps revenue $60.4B (+28%); FoA ad revenue $59.4B (+27%, +26% cc). Impressions +14% (all regions; engagement + users + ad-load optimization); price per ad +12% (performance gains, macro, FX). FoA OTHER revenue crossed $1 BILLION for the first time, +73% — WhatsApp paid messaging + subscriptions. Reality Labs $431M (+16%; AI glasses up, Quest down). Total revenue $60.8B (+28%). Total expenses $42B (+55%), incl. $2.4B legal charges + $1.2B severance (May-2026 reduction of ~8,000 roles); growth driven by comp (AI talent), infrastructure (depreciation, DC opex, THIRD-PARTY CLOUD SPEND) and THIRD-PARTY AI TOKEN COSTS. Headcount 75,000+ (-3% QoQ). Operating income $18.8B (31% margin, -8% YoY; +9% ex legal/severance). Tax 16%; net income $15.8B; EPS $6.18. CAPEX incl. finance leases $31.1B. FREE CASH FLOW $784 MILLION. Cash $90.3B; debt $83.7B.

Engagement: Instagram global time spent +double digits; Facebook video time +9% (+10%+ US/Canada). Every public IG Reels/Feed post is auto-processed through an LLM (topics to tone) — key personalization building block; Muse models now doing content understanding (video topic classification/summarization). Largest single Reels ranking release ever (+15bps sessions; deeper user history + faster inference), coming to Feed. On IG Feed, OVER HALF of recommended content is <1 DAY OLD (2x+ vs a year ago). 'Your Algo' (IG) + 'Shape Your Feed' (FB; >80% retention among engagers). Research milestone: continuously pre-trained a large-scale model with RECOMMENDATIONS DATA and observed HEALTHY SCALING LAWS — foundation models to power organic + ads recommendations simultaneously; LLM-native recommenders next.

Monetization: Threads global ads expansion COMPLETE; WhatsApp Status ads expanding toward global rollout. META GENERATIVE RECOMMENDER — 'a paradigm shift': LLMs reason about ad content and user preferences together rather than scoring every ad individually; first generative model deployed in ads retrieval with notable performance gains; LLM user-preference pilots +1% app-event conversions (IG). GEM ranking + sequence learning + user-understanding: +8.3% AD CLICKS and +15.7% CONVERSIONS on Facebook. Advantage+ end-to-end: >$75 BILLION annual revenue run rate. AI creative: image-generation adoption more than DOUBLED QoQ; 9M SMBs. Meta Business Agent Platform launched (enterprise-grade agents on WhatsApp); Movida (Brazil, ~400 locations): +44% daily WhatsApp bookings, 85% of conversations resolved entirely by the agent. Muse Spark on OpenRouter (US devs); model API to more channels/countries/enterprises soon.

CAPACITY PHILOSOPHY: industry has UNDER-BUILT historically — existing capacity 'extremely valuable'; supply chains need build-out for anticipated needs. Plans geared to MAXIMIZING 2026 AND 2027 CAPACITY ('near-term capacity is more valuable than long-term capacity'); 2028+ strategy = FLEXIBILITY — lay data-center and network foundations (land + power) now, make SERVER decisions later. 'We are today and expect to be in the foreseeable future DEMAND-CONSTRAINED — that includes our core business, where we still have numerous ROI-positive places we would put compute toward if we had it.' Custom silicon (MTIA) = long-term strategic flexibility and SUPPLY-CHAIN LEVERAGE. Industry capacity to remain TIGHT for the foreseeable future. Enterprise offerings may take multiple forms: agentic tools, API, or MONETIZING COMPUTE DIRECTLY given outsized market demand. BlackRock venture = example partnership structure for funding.

Outlook: Q3 revenue $61-64B (~1% FX headwind). FY2026 expenses $165-169B (low end raised for the legal charge). 2026 operating income still expected ABOVE 2025. FY2026 CAPEX NARROWED TO $130-145B (from $125-145B). Tax 15-17%. Youth-related trials scheduled this year — possible material loss.

[Q&A highlights]
- Brian Nowak (MS): which scales first? Zuckerberg: substantial compute to frontier training; rest across core optimization, new consumer products, API, business agents, developer tools, and DIRECT COMPUTE SALES — 'meaningful growth in ALL of these areas.' Susan: NO 2027 capex outlook yet; highly dynamic; near-term capacity worth more.
- Eric Sheridan (GS): enterprise go-to-market: business agents = natural extension of the ad/SMB sales motion (pay-on-results auction); coding/productivity tools = 'a somewhat different muscle... a very large opportunity'; enterprise = SUM of compute + API + productivity + agents. Susan: capital = operating cash flow + growing debt mix + partnerships (BlackRock model) to lower cost of capital.
- Mark Shmulik (Bernstein): consumer AI-utility gap — coding was first agentic market (technical users, closed-loop); Meta's bet = consumer personal agents that 'just work' for billions; 'almost inevitable someone does this' and it plays to Meta's scaling strength.
- Doug Anmuth (JPM): recsys roadmap — more personalization, faster response, longer user-interaction sequences, bigger architectures, LLM content understanding into recommendations + policy enforcement, agentic ranking engineers. Zuckerberg on buy-vs-sell compute: 'there's just NOWHERE NEAR ENOUGH COMPUTE FOR ALL THE DEMAND'; portfolio: don't sell it all for short-term profit — intelligence on top compounds the value; data centers have lead times, demand is very large.
- Justin Post (BofA): MSL trajectory strong; advantages = data flywheels per product + best-in-world scaling to billions + culture.
- Ross Sandler (Barclays): scaling ladder — released models impressive for their scale; LARGER models in training; efficient models serve billions (most prompts), advanced models for hard problems. OPEN SOURCE: 'we expect we will get back to releasing some open source models at some point soon' — mix of open and closed continues.
- Ken Gawrelski (WF): can't rely on others' open-weight models (not frontier-strength; reliance risk); Meta = FULL-STACK company (own DCs, chips, low-level software) — model sovereignty is part of the stack; models have different skill spikes; full-stack tuning = durable advantage. Susan: 'maximizing 26/27 capacity' = demand statement (demand-constrained incl. core) + long-term supply-chain uncertainty; 2028 planning = flexibility (land/power now, chip decisions later).

[End of call]
