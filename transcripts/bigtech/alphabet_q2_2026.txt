[Sundar Pichai — CEO]
Alphabet revenue +24%. Search & other +17%; YouTube ads +13%; CLOUD REVENUE GREW 82%, powered by strong demand for AI infrastructure and AI solutions. CLOUD BACKLOG GREW TO $514 BILLION. Nearly 90% of Fortune 100 using Gemini Enterprise.

Models: announced Gemini 3.6 Flash and 3.5 Flash-Lite (cost-effective workhorses); Gemini 3.5 Flash Cyber + CodeMender agent (finds and fixes vulnerabilities); Gemini 3.5 Pro in testing; STARTED THE MOST AMBITIOUS PRE-TRAINING RUN YET FOR GEMINI 4. 'We continue to be SUPPLY CONSTRAINED.' >9M developers building monthly; model APIs processing ~22 BILLION TOKENS PER MINUTE, UP FROM 16 BILLION A QUARTER AGO. Omni (create anything from any input, starting video); Gemini app video DAU +40% since I/O. Gemma open models >900M downloads (Gemma 4 >300M since April). Antigravity agentic dev platform >2.4M weekly actives; internal example: Chrome team compressing a two-year timeline into three months (8x) via model-driven refactoring.

Search: AI Overviews + AI Mode merged into one experience; AI Mode >1 BILLION MAU since global expansion last October; AI features send billions of clicks to websites weekly; cost of AI Mode responses reduced to LOWEST LEVEL SINCE LAUNCH (engineering + hardware optimizations). Gemini app 950M MAU, DAU TRIPLED in a year. YouTube: 1.7B unique viewers of World Cup content; Ask YouTube 140M users engaged in June.

Cloud: integrated AI portfolio (chips, models, data, security, agents). New-customer acquisition velocity MORE THAN DOUBLED YoY; existing customers EXCEEDING COMMITMENTS BY >50% (accelerating); Marketplace transactions 7x YoY. Agent Development Kit ~70M downloads. Nearly 500 Cloud customers each processed >1 TRILLION tokens in the last year; >2,000 enterprises consumed >100 BILLION tokens in 12 months. Security: 90% of Fortune 100 are Google Cloud security users; Wiz AI-security features ~90% attach; AI workloads scanned +45% QoQ.

AI infrastructure: broadest accelerator range from Google and NVIDIA — including the NEW NVIDIA VERA RUBIN PLATFORM and TPU 8t/8i. Three unique offerings: (1) VIRGO NETWORK — connect A MILLION AI ACCELERATORS across multiple data-center sites into a unified supercomputer; (2) software stack with native JAX/PyTorch/vLLM/SGLang for GPU<->TPU workload portability; (3) new AGENT-OPTIMIZED Google AXION CPU: 30% better performance per dollar vs peers. AI-infra customers: Ineffable Intelligence (leading lab), Kakao, Deutsche Börse, Pfizer, Roche, World Labs. Other bets: Waymo Ojai vehicle (6th-gen Driver); Wing >1M deliveries; Isomorphic Labs raised >$2B.

Services revenues $95B (+15%). Search & other $63.3B (+17%; retail + finance led). YouTube ads +13% ($11.1B). Gemini across ads infrastructure: shopping ads 20% improvement in showing highly relevant ads; AI Max out of beta with 500,000 ADVERTISERS (avg +15% conversions/value at similar ROAS); >half of SMBs use AI creative tools. [Anat Ashkenazi — CFO]
12th consecutive quarter of double-digit growth. Revenues $119.8B (+24%, +23% cc). TAC $16.2B (+10%). Opex +27%; R&D +32% (AI talent + depreciation). Operating income $40.8B (+30%), margin 34%. OI&E: large unrealized equity-portfolio gains (stated '$98 billion' on the call — treat with caution; marketable equity securities total $87.1B). Operating cash flow $39.1B (TTM $185.7B). CAPEX $44.9 BILLION IN THE QUARTER — ~60% SERVERS, ~40% DATA CENTERS + NETWORKING. NEGATIVE FREE CASH FLOW OF $5.9B in Q2; TTM FCF $53.3B. Cash + marketable securities $242.5B (incl. $87.1B equity securities); long-term debt $98.2B (debt portfolio expanded from ~$16B to ~$100B in 12 months). Dividend $0.22.

Services: op income $39.5B, margin 41.8%. Cloud: revenues $24.8B (+82%), led by GCP (grew faster than Cloud overall) across core GCP, AI solutions, AI infrastructure. BEGAN RECOGNIZING REVENUE FROM TPU SYSTEM SALES — DELIVERED TO CUSTOMER DATA CENTERS FOR THE FIRST TIME IN Q2. Cloud growth 'accelerated meaningfully EVEN AFTER EXCLUDING the impact of TPU system sales.' Cloud op income $8.8B — MORE THAN TRIPLED YoY; margin 20.7% -> 35.6%. BACKLOG +>$50B sequentially to $514B (majority typical GCP contracts; TPU system sales agreements also reflected); just over 50% to be recognized as revenue over the next 24 months. TPU system sales: relatively small portion of revenue in 2026, ramping as 2026 exits; VAST MAJORITY OF REVENUE REALIZED IN 2027 (inventory built ahead — visible in cost of revenues).

Outlook: supply-constrained; Q3 will EXPAND USE OF THIRD-PARTY CAPACITY AS A BRIDGING STRATEGY while internal capacity builds — modest near-term Cloud margin pressure. FULL-YEAR 2026 CAPEX GUIDANCE RAISED TO $195-205 BILLION (from $180-190B) — 'acceleration in the delivery of capacity to meet growing demand'; 2027 CAPEX TO INCREASE SIGNIFICANTLY. FCF to remain under pressure. Hiring continues in AI/Cloud.

[Q&A highlights]
- Brian Nowak (MS): ROIC view more bullish than a year ago; enterprises 'barely scratching' AI-native workloads. Anat: multi-year capacity planning; demand still outpaces the significant capacity added over 3 years; full-stack lets Google drive efficiency to deliver more compute.
- Doug Anmuth (JPM): frontier confidence — Gemini 3.6 Flash +10 points on DeepSuite vs 3.5, more token-efficient, 6-week iteration; GEMINI 4 needed as larger base model to compete at next frontier; 'very excited by internal progress.' Capital structure: cash from ops first, then debt ($16B->$100B), equity raise for resilience; NOT planning to return to equity markets (ATM only for SBC tax).
- Eric Sheridan (GS): TPU allocation priorities: (1) frontier AGI development first — 'the foundation for everything'; (2) core products (Search/YouTube) + serving models for Cloud (Vertex, Gemini Enterprise); (3) external infrastructure demand balanced by PUTTING TPUs IN CUSTOMERS' OR OTHER DATA CENTERS — 'like the project we are doing with BLACKSTONE.' Anat: TPU sale agreements enter cloud backlog when signed; inventory built ahead (hits cash from ops); revenue on delivery; small in 2026, vast majority 2027.
- Ross Sandler (Barclays): cites 'the third-party compute deal with SPACEX' (analyst's words; management discussed third-party bridging without naming). Sundar: full 'parade of frontier' — best models at every price point; model-release pace picking up toward ~MONTHLY cadence as Gemini 4 lands.
- Michael Nathanson: moats = full-stack solutions (models just an ingredient in cyber/data-analytics/agents), compute to train + serve, data quality, confidentiality guarantees, orchestrated agentic workflows. Anat on TPU margins: no product-level breakout; 'benefits from designing and manufacturing our own chips'; TPU sales EXPAND TAM; Q3 Cloud margin pressure from third-party bridge capacity + Wiz integration.
- Mark Shmulik (Bernstein): allocation trade-offs: AGI frontier first, then Search/YouTube/Cloud serving. On bridge deals: 'very, very large customers... a short-term cost over a few months may be very high, but in the lifetime of the deal highly ROI positive' — willing to take an upfront six-month cost to serve a multi-year opportunity.
- Ron Josey (Citi): Search 17% growth drivers — Gemini across ads stack; AI Max unlocking billions of net-new previously unmonetizable searches.
- Ken Gawrelski (Wells Fargo): 2027 returns — input-cost inflation reflected in pricing; demand indicators (long-term deals, renewals with exceptional demand) healthier than a year ago. Waymo: focused on scaling within Alphabet's bet structure.
- Shweta Khajuria (Wolfe): TPU long-term — will scale TPU external systems per demand within constraints and internal allocation needs; no far-future projection of a merchant-silicon business.

[End of call]
