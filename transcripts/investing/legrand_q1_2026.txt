Legrand reported its Q1 2026 earnings, revealing a mixed financial performance. The company posted an earnings per share (EPS) of $1.26, falling short of the forecasted $1.32, marking a 4.55% negative surprise. Despite this, Legrand exceeded revenue expectations, reporting $2.54 billion against a $2.49 billion forecast. The market reacted with a 2.42% drop in pre-market trading, bringing the stock price down to $156.58 from $160.45.
