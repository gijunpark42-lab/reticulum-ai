Advantest said its first quarter of fiscal 2026 delivered record sales, operating income and net income, prompting the Japanese semiconductor test-equipment maker to raise its full-year outlook. The company tied the stronger results to rising demand from artificial intelligence, especially inference AI, which is increasing the need for semiconductor testing across ASICs, CPUs and DRAM. Shares were last down 1.22% at $25,200, compared with a previous close of $25,510, after trading in a 52-week range of $9,744 to $35,940.

Advantest said the quarter marked a very strong start to fiscal 2026. The company described the period as one in which all major financial measures reached all-time highs. Management said the results reflect a structural shift in semiconductor demand, with AI-related devices requiring more testing content per chip.

The company&rsquo;s comments suggest that the current growth cycle is not being driven only by unit volume. Instead, Advantest said each new generation of chips is becoming more complex and needs more test insertions. That trend supports demand for the company&rsquo;s equipment and related solutions.

The company also said its business is evolving beyond test hardware. It is investing in software, automation, data analytics and other adjacent offerings, which it believes will strengthen its position over the medium to long term.

The available forecast called for revenue of 335.34 billion, but no actual revenue or EPS figures were provided in the materials reviewed. That means a precise beat or miss cannot be calculated here.

Even so, management&rsquo;s tone was clearly upbeat. The company said Q1 results set records across sales, operating income and net income, and it raised full-year guidance as a result. That combination usually signals that performance came in ahead of internal expectations or that the outlook improved materially during the quarter.

The company also said the tester market in 2026 is now expected to be larger than previously thought, with both SoC and memory tester demand running ahead of April expectations. That suggests the quarter likely strengthened confidence in the business outlook.

Advantest shares were last at $25,200, down 1.22% from the previous close of $25,510. The decline of $310 came after the earnings update, though the stock&rsquo;s exact pre- and post-call path was not provided.
