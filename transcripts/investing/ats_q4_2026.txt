AT&S reported robust financial results for the fiscal year 2025/2026, showcasing a significant turnaround. The company achieved a 21% revenue growth in constant currency, reaching 1.8 billion euros, despite facing substantial foreign exchange headwinds. The stock price reacted positively, rising 1.31% to 116.1 euros, reflecting investor confidence in the company’s strategic direction and financial health.

AT&S achieved a 21% revenue growth in constant currency for FY 2025/2026.EBITDA margin improved significantly, up 560 basis points from the previous year.The company turned operating free cash flow positive, swinging from a negative 498 million euros to a positive 236 million euros.Strong performance in the Microelectronics segment, with 53% growth driven by AI infrastructure investments.Strategic expansion and innovation in AI and high-power electronics sectors.

Company PerformanceAT&S demonstrated a remarkable performance in fiscal year 2025/2026, reversing previous losses and achieving profitability in the last two quarters. The company reported a total revenue of 1.8 billion euros, marking a 21% increase from the previous year. This growth was driven by strong demand in the Microelectronics business unit and successful cost optimization efforts. The company’s strategic focus on AI infrastructure and advanced computing systems has positioned it as a key player in the rapidly growing market.

Revenue: 1.8 billion euros, up 21% in constant currency from the previous year.EBITDA: 418 million euros, with a margin of 23.3%, a 49% improvement.EBIT: 66 million euros, a 238% increase.Operating Free Cash Flow: Positive 236 million euros, a significant turnaround.Net Debt/EBITDA: Improved from 5.3x to 3.2x.

Outlook & GuidanceAT&S provided an optimistic outlook for fiscal year 2026/2027, with revenue guidance set between 2.1 billion and 2.4 billion euros. The company anticipates 30%-35% growth, driven by strong demand from both existing and new customers. The focus remains on expanding capacity and leveraging positive pricing trends, particularly in the Microelectronics segment.

CEO Michael Mertin emphasized the company’s transition from a traditional PCB provider to an innovation partner, highlighting its comprehensive technology roadmap for AI infrastructure. He stated, "Our strategic investments in AI and high-power electronics are yielding significant returns, positioning AT&S as a leader in the market."

Supply Chain Constraints: Ongoing shortages in T-glass and other materials could impact production schedules and costs.Market Competition: Increasing competition in the AI infrastructure space may pressure margins.Economic Uncertainty: Macroeconomic factors, including currency fluctuations and inflation, could affect financial performance.Capacity Limitations: Current production capacity may not meet the growing demand, necessitating further investments.

AT&S’s strong financial performance and strategic focus on innovation and expansion have positioned it well for future growth, despite potential challenges in the macroeconomic environment.
