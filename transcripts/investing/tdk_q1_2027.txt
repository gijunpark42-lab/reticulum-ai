TDK reported record first-quarter results for fiscal 2027, with net sales rising 38.3% from a year earlier and operating profit climbing 53%, driven by strong demand from AI data centers, smartphones and batteries. The company said profit at every level reached a first-quarter high. The stock rose 6.99% to $3,008, up from a previous close of $2,811.5, as investors reacted to the stronger-than-expected operating trend and upbeat guidance for the next quarter.

TDK delivered a broad-based improvement across its main businesses in the quarter ended June 2026, with growth coming from both industrial and consumer-linked markets. The company said demand for nearline hard-disk drives used in AI data centers remained robust, while newly launched smartphone models helped offset weaker ICT production caused by tight memory supply and higher memory prices.

All four major segments posted year-on-year sales growth. Passive Components sales rose 28% to JPY 176.8 billion. Sensor Application Products increased 33.3% to JPY 61.9 billion. Magnetic Application Products jumped 49.6% to JPY 81.6 billion. Energy Application Products, the largest segment, climbed 42.1% to JPY 405.8 billion.

The results suggest TDK is benefiting from several secular themes at once, including AI infrastructure spending, automotive electrification and industrial demand recovery. The company also said its MEMS sensor business returned to profitability, while quality improvements and cost reductions narrowed losses in the magnet business. The strong performance is reflected in TDK’s return on equity of 10% and revenue growth of 13.6% over the last twelve months, according to InvestingPro data. The key highlights of the first quarter results for the fiscal year ending March 2027. In the automotive market, demand remained resilient, supported by the continued shift toward electrification and autonomous driving. Against this backdrop, although lower production of ICT-related products had a negative impact, strong sales of newly launched smartphone models, together with solid demand related to AI data centers, drove year-on-year growth in both sales and operating profit across all business segments.

Overall, net sales increased by 38.3%, while operating profit rose 53%. Both net sales and operating profit reached record highs for the first quarter. Next, the first quarter financial results in detail. Profit before tax was JPY 94.5 billion, up JPY 36.9 billion, or 64% from earlier. Profit attributable to owners of the parent reached JPY 80.6 billion, up by 94.94%. We achieved record highs in net sales at every level of profit. Quarterly EPS came to JPY 42.45.

Our operating profit sensitivity exchange rate is estimated at around JPY 2 billion annually for every one yen movement against the US dollar and approximately EUR 300 million against the euro. Sales for AI data center applications increased significantly. Net sales reached JPY 176.8 billion, up 28% year-on-year. Operating profit totaled JPY 17.4 billion, approximately 2.7 times. Aluminum film capacitors benefited from higher sales to both the automotive market and AI data centers. Inductive devices posted higher sales and profits supported by increased automotive demand. Although sales of high-frequency components declined in the automotive and ICT markets, however, profitability improved.

Piezoelectric materials and circuit protection devices recorded higher sales and profits thanks to increased demand from the industrial equipment and automotive markets. Sensor Application Products. Operating profit reached JPY 7.8 billion, nearly three times the level of the previous year. Temperature and pressure sensors posted higher sales and profits, supported by stronger demand from the automotive and industrial equipment markets. Magnetic sensors benefited from increased smartphone demand for TMR sensors, as well as higher industrial equipment demand for Hall sensors. As a result, the magnetic sensor business achieved higher sales and profits. MEMS sensors benefited from higher sales of motion sensors for both ICT and industrial equipment applications. The MEMS sensor business achieved higher sales and returned to profitability from a loss a year earlier.

As a result, both sales and profit increased substantially. Profits declined because the previous year included approximately JPY 1 billion in one-time gain. Ongoing quality improvements and cost reductions significantly narrowed the underlying loss. Energy Application Products. Net sales reached JPY 405.8 billion, an increase of 42.1%. Operating profit came to JPY 69.4 billion, up 25.3%. Shipment volumes of rechargeable batteries declined as production of ICT-related products decreased. Power supplies for industrial equipment benefited from a gradual recovery in demand, particularly from semiconductors. Both sales and profits increased.
