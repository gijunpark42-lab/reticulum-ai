Vishay Intertechnology ( VSH +0.01% ) demonstrated sequential and year-over-year revenue growth, supported by strong order momentum and broad-based end market advances. Management cited increased market share in automotive, significant capacity expansion, and a meaningful build in backlog and book-to-bill, showing stronger demand visibility across all product lines. The company detailed that its aggressive investment in high-growth products—especially for AI, automotive, and industrial segments—is driving measurable returns, with tangible operating leverage evidenced in enhanced gross and EBITDA margins. Strategic capacity expansion remains central, with management linking sustained margin achievement to planned completion of restructuring and the new German fab ramp. Free cash flow will likely remain negative due to front-loaded capital expenditures, yet the policy to return at least 70% of free cash flow to shareholders is unchanged, though actual share buybacks are deferred until higher liquidity is restored.
