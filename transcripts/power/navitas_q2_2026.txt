THE FOUR 800V INFLECTION POINTS (the framework; slides posted):
1. H2-2026 ramp, accelerating H1-2027: SiC replaces silicon in AC/DC PSUs on POWER/DENSITY SCALING ALONE — independent of 800V, even with 50V DC output. Happening NOW.
2. Mid-2027 (±400V variant EARLIER — H1-2027, tied to certain XPUs/ASICs): 800V BUSBAR IN THE SIDECAR RACK — AC/DC power shelves + BBUs move from the IT rack to the power sidecar with 800V DC output; adds high-voltage SiC in higher-power AC/DC PSUs (18/23/27/30 kW) + NEW SiC and GaN content in top-of-rack DC/DC PSUs (15-30 kW) and BBUs. Advanced system design + LARGE-QUANTITY system-level reliability testing underway with several key customers; multiple platforms, multiple hyperscalers, multiple merchant-power vendors per hyperscaler.
3. Mid-to-late 2027, accelerating late-2027/early-2028: NATIVE 800V — high-density DC/DC integrated INTO the GPU/XPU trays using GaN (switching frequency + power density); 'a fundamental change in rack power architecture'; ramps IN STEPS across multiple GPUs/XPUs/hyperscalers at various times — NOT one switch, NOT one customer.
4. 2028+: SOLID-STATE TRANSFORMERS — mid-voltage AC straight to 800V DC on site; ultra-high-voltage SiC + GaN grid-to-core.

On the 800V-delay chatter / NVIDIA Kyber cancellation reports (Quinn Bolton q): won't comment on NVIDIA's plans, but 'there is a MISCONCEPTION in the 800V being a DIGITAL SWITCH' — inflection 2 already starts 800V via the sidecar; inflection 3 spreads across multiple platforms in steps through 2027-2028. NO CHANGE to the outlook. Having BOTH GaN and SiC is the strategic advantage (some platforms use both — e.g. DC/DC PSUs and BBUs).

Product/tech: 800V-to-6V GaN DC-DC board demoed (800V-to-12V in development); new secondary-side efficiency programs for 800V topologies; DFN 8x8 dual-side-cooled package adoption; 650V 11 mOhm GaN FET = industry's lowest-RDS(on) HV GaN, significant customers preparing mass production; 100V medium-voltage GaN growing. SiC: GeneSiC trench-assisted planar; NEW Isolated TO-247 family (1.2-3.3kV, module-like performance, direct/liquid cooling); 1.2kV JFET line (release early 2027) targeting eFuse/ORing/SOLID-STATE CIRCUIT BREAKERS in AI DC + SST + grid = ~$1B (up to $1.3B) INCREMENTAL SAM by 2030 — one SST customer's addressable content rose 40% with JFETs added; 6.5kV SiC technology releasing in Q3 (unveil 'very soon'); 10kV SiC in development WITH A PROMINENT LEAD CUSTOMER (announcement in coming weeks). Grid/energy: 2.3kV/3.3kV GeneSiC modules getting volume-sample requests for H2 system tests (BESS, solar PCS, SSTs) — grid ramps start 2027, SST jump 2028.

MAGNACHIP PARTNERSHIP (announced last week): LICENSES GeneSiC Gen4/Gen5 trench-assisted planar technology (1.2/2.3/3.3kV and higher) — to be qualified/internalized in Magnachip's South Korea fab. Two goals: (1) SAM expansion into markets Navitas does NOT serve; (2) a SECOND FOUNDRY SOURCE for Navitas SiC wafers (supply-chain resilience at 60-70% SiC CAGR). Not primarily a royalty story — 'we're not creating a competitor, we're creating an extension of Navitas.'

Operations: GlobalFoundries 8-INCH GaN pivot ON TRACK — lead parts to FULL CUSTOMER SAMPLING/QUALIFICATION BEFORE YEAR-END, first qualified product EARLY 2027 (US-based GaN for national-security + resilience); TSMC BUFFER CAPACITY SECURED for smooth customer transition 'throughout 2029 and beyond' (visible in +$4.6M inventory to $19.5M plus ~$15M prepaid wafers); OSAT consolidation to fewer strategic partners.
