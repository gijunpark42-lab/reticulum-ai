=== HEADLINE FINANCIALS ===
Q4 FY2026 (quarter ended March 31, 2026):
- Net sales: $954.4 million, up $307.2M / +47% YoY (record quarterly sales)
- Net earnings: $73.6 million, +$23.5M / +47% YoY
- GAAP diluted EPS: $1.36, +$0.44 / +48% YoY
- Adjusted EPS: $1.71, +$0.59 / +53% YoY
- Adjusted EBITDA: $146.1 million, +$42.0M / +40% YoY

Full-year FY2026 (ended March 31, 2026):
- Net sales: $3,181.1 million, +$597.6M / +23% YoY (vs $2,583.5M prior year)
- Climate Solutions segment sales: $2,062.3 million, +$621.5M / +43% YoY
- Adjusted EBITDA: $471.0 million, +$78.9M / +20% YoY
- Adjusted EPS: $5.02, +$0.97 / +24% YoY

=== DATA CENTER ===
- Q4 data center revenue: over $400 million, +158% YoY
- Full-year FY2026 data center sales: $1.1 billion, +73% YoY
- North American chiller production increased fivefold (5x) versus the prior year
- $100 million investment to expand US data center product capacity (announced in fiscal Q2) is firmly on schedule; first chillers shipped from Jefferson City, Missouri, and air-handling units from Wisconsin facilities

=== LANDMARK LONG-TERM AGREEMENT (LTA) ===
- Signed with a key strategic data center customer (existing, not new)
- $4 billion of data center cooling products (chillers) during calendar years 2027 through 2029
- Received an upfront cash payment of $165 million, structured as a contract liability recognized over the life of the agreement
- Revenue distribution projected at no more than $2 billion a year; partial revenue captured in Q4 FY2026

=== FISCAL 2027 GUIDANCE ===
- Net sales growth: +20% to +35%
- Adjusted EBITDA: $650 million to $680 million (+38% to +44%)
- Data center sales growth: +60% to +80% in fiscal 2027 (upgraded from prior 50-70% multi-year estimate); fiscal 2028 reverts to the 50-70% growth-rate range, with the LTA providing elevated near-term visibility

=== PERFORMANCE TECHNOLOGIES / GENTHERM SPIN-OFF ===
- Planned separation of the Performance Technologies segment and merger with Gentherm remains on track for completion before the end of calendar year 2026
- Contingent on S-4 submission, shareholder approval, and receipt of an IRS determination letter
- Modine to receive ~$210M cash + Modine shareholders receive 40% equity of the combined business (tax-free distribution); values PT at ~$1B (announced prior quarter)
- Completes Modine's transformation into substantially a data-center / commercial-HVAC thermal pure-play
