STMicroelectronics Q1 2026 Earnings Call — FULL TRANSCRIPT (provided by user)
Results announced 04-23-2026. Speakers: Jean-Marc Chery (President & CEO), Lorenzo Grandi (President & CFO), Marco Cassis (President APMS / Head of Strategy), Jérôme Ramel (IR).

=== PREPARED REMARKS — CEO ===
Q1 net revenues $3.1B, including ~$40M from NXP's MEMS sensor business acquired during the quarter (Feb). Excluding it, revenue above the midpoint of guidance, driven by engaged customer programs in personal electronics and comms equipment & computer peripherals. GM 33.8% (34.1% ex-PPA). Non-GAAP diluted EPS $0.13. Balance-sheet inventory up slightly; DISTRIBUTION INVENTORIES NOW NORMALIZED. FCF -$720M including $895M cash out for the NXP MEMS acquisition.

Business dynamics: STRONG booking momentum — book-to-bill WELL ABOVE 1 across ALL end markets and regions.
- Automotive: -10% QoQ, +15% YoY (return to YoY growth). Design wins across EV/hybrid/traditional: onboard chargers, DC/DC, powertrain, active suspension, vehicle control; power semis, smart power, auto MCUs, analog, sensors. NXP MEMS integration on plan (complementary; strengthens auto sensing).
- Industrial: -1% QoQ, +26% YoY; distribution inventory normalized. Design wins in automation/robotics, building automation, power systems, healthcare, appliances. Announced collaboration with NVIDIA to integrate ST sensors, MCUs and motor-control with the NVIDIA robotics ecosystem (humanoid robots / physical AI). Ranked #1 worldwide in general-purpose MCUs for the 5th consecutive year (Omdia). First STM32 wafers FULLY PRODUCED IN CHINA by partner Wayon delivered to Chinese customers (China-for-China strategy).
- Personal electronics: -14% QoQ (seasonality of engaged programs), +21% YoY on content. Motion sensing + secure NFC on Qualcomm's new personal AI platform.
- Comms equipment & computer peripherals: +3% QoQ (above expectations), +41% YoY. ST powers, cools and connects AI data centers from grid to core and core to user. CONFIRMED data-center revenue "nicely above $500M" for 2026 and "well above $1B" for 2027.
- MAJOR: expanded strategic engagement with AMAZON WEB SERVICES — a MULTI-YEAR, MULTI-BILLION-DOLLAR commercial engagement to enable new high-performance compute infrastructure for cloud and AI data centers, covering a broad range of ST proprietary technologies.
- Multiple design wins for silicon and SiC power solutions for next-gen AI compute power density.
- Expanded 800VDC AI data-center power-conversion portfolio with NEW 12V AND 6V ARCHITECTURES IN COLLABORATION WITH NVIDIA — complete portfolio for 800VDC distribution in gigawatt-scale compute (power, analog/mixed-signal, MCU).
- Silicon photonics PIC100 platform: START OF HIGH-VOLUME PRODUCTION — used by hyperscalers for optical interconnect for DCs and AI clusters. Optical-interconnect momentum also drives high-performance MCU demand in pluggable optics. Initial Secure Element demand in data-server PSUs (authentication / anti-manipulation).
- LEO satellites (BiCMOS + panel-level packaging): strong progress; selected by main LEO customer to develop a power-amplifier controller for DIRECT-TO-CELL satellites (proprietary BCD); ramping shipments to the second-largest customer. Ambition: WELL ABOVE $3B cumulative LEO revenue 2026-2028 (dedicated call May 4).
- Sustainability: carbon-neutral 2027 (scope 1+2, partial 3) on track; 86% renewable electricity in 2025 (target 100% by 2027).

Q1 operating income $70M GAAP (incl $71M impairment/restructuring + $30M PPA); non-GAAP OI $171M (5.5%). Segment op margins: AM&S 12.2%, P&D -21.5%, EP 16.9%, RF&OC 14.9%. === Q2 2026 OUTLOOK ===
Revenue $3.45B ±3.5% (+11.6% QoQ, +24.9% YoY). GM ~34.8% ±200bps (incl ~100bps unused-capacity charges); non-GAAP GM ~35.2%. No tariff changes assumed. Q2 well above average seasonality. FY2026: DOUBLE-DIGIT revenue growth, driven by addressable-market dynamics, engaged programs, and NEW AI PROGRAMS.

=== Q&A KEY POINTS ===
- Shape of year: strong Q1 bookings show NO pull-ins; well-balanced loading; ~85-90% of Q1 bookings billable in 2026; confident H2 at usual seasonality vs H1. 2026 growth drivers: auto (ADAS, sensors incl NXP MEMS boost, SiC), industrial (GP MCU strong), PE (engaged programs, smaller in H2 on new-device profile change), DATA CENTER (strong acceleration incl. cloud optical interconnect: PIC100 + BiCMOS + GP MCU + analog + power discrete). Only negative: capacity-reservation fees -$140M YoY.
- Pricing: was guiding low-to-mid single-digit decline; Q1 declined low-single-digit as expected; NOW seeing selected price increases (April hikes) -> full-year expectation = VERY LOW single-digit decline. Pricing neutral to Q2 GM bridge (mix positive, unused charges lower, manufacturing efficiency temporarily suboptimal from fab transitions 200->300mm and SiC 150->200mm).
- GM trajectory: sequential improvement each quarter of 2026; drivers = lower unused-capacity charges (won't fully disappear — legacy tech pockets), progressive efficiency (full transition benefits mainly 2027), positive mix; H2 may see higher input costs (energy, ~6-month lag from crude). Path: GM 40% at >$4B quarterly revenue after reshaping completes.
- Q2 upside driver: optical already contributing via high-performance MCUs since Q1; photonics/BiCMOS bulk comes later.
- AWS & hyperscalers (midterm): three domains — NETWORK FLOW (optical cable, CPO/pluggables; AWS a main driver but ST positioned far beyond AWS), POWER FLOW (20,000V -> 0.8V; SPS, LV MOSFETs, MCU, drivers, sensing; will compete far beyond AWS), THERMAL/COOLING (power + MCU + analog). "ST is a unique company able to provide photonics, MEMS (coming soon), MCU, power switches, drivers, controllers and sensors on this infrastructure."
- AI capacity: photonics & associated tech in RAMP-UP mode; unconstrained 2026/2027 demand is ABOVE the >$500M / >$1B figures; ramping installed capacity, adding more in H2; aim to fulfill as much unconstrained demand as possible; more color in July.
- DC 2026 revenue split: ~40% analog & power, ~60% MCU + RF & optical comms.
- Reshaping timing: Agrate 300mm full speed end-2027 into 2028 (customer qualification paced). SiC 6->8 inch similar; key European EV platform customer. Sanan JV (Chongqing): production loading starts END-2026.
- NXP MEMS: monocrystalline-silicon accelerometers (excellent temperature performance) + ST 6-axis; auto safety; historically ~low-single-digit growth, expected to accelerate above market.
- GaN: important for 800V; sockets come to life this year and next; ecosystem = Taiwan PSU makers + US ecosystem; ST portfolio now covers all power-conversion stages.
- Photonics: PIC100 maybe "application standard specific" rather than standard product; ST claims unique 300mm silicon-photonics capability (Crolles, possibly Agrate later); NPO/CPO innovations coming "maybe faster than expected" — silicon photonics is the key enabler.
