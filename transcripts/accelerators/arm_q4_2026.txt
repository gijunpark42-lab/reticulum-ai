Arm Holdings plc (ARM +1.03%) delivered all-time high quarterly and full-year revenues alongside record non-GAAP profitability, with performance underpinned by surging demand in Cloud AI, data center, and next-generation licensing. Management reported rapidly escalating Arm AGI CPU demand, more than doubling initial launch expectations, and highlighted broad ecosystem support from both platform partners and end users. Momentum in royalty revenue was concentrated in Cloud AI and data center, where Arm-based technologies are becoming central to architecture among major hyperscalers, including during recent high-profile product rollouts. Management confirmed continued expansion of operating expenses to support R&D and customer delivery but kept guidance unchanged in light of supply chain capacity limitations. Strategic clarity centered on a dual growth vector model, with silicon and IP both projected to drive structural acceleration through the end of the decade.
