## Item 5.02
Item 5.02 Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.




Effective August 25, 2026, Ms. Jennifer Fritzsche resigned from the Board of Directors of Dycom Industries, Inc. (the “Company”). Accordingly, on August 25, 2026, Ms. Fritzsche’s resignation became effective and the size of the Board was reduced from eleven members to ten members. Ms. Fritzsche’s resignation from the Board was not the result of any disagreement with the Company or any matter relating to the Company’s operations, policies, or practices.
