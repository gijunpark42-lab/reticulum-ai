## Item 2.02
Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition. 




On May 28, 2026, Dell Technologies Inc. (the “Company” or “Dell”) issued a press release announcing its financial results for its fiscal quarter ended May 1, 2026. A copy of the press release is furnished as Exhibit 99.1 to this current report.
