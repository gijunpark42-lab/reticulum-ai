## Item 8.01
Item 8.01 | Other Events. | 



 On August 4, 2026, Entergy Corporation (the “Company”) entered into an Underwriting Agreement for the sale of $750,000,000 in aggregate principal amount of the Company’s Junior Subordinated Debentures due December 15, 2056 (Series 2026A) (the “Series 2026A Junior Subordinated Debentures”) and $750,000,000 in aggregate principal amount of the Company’s Junior Subordinated Debentures due December 15, 2058 (Series 2026B) (the “Series 2026B Junior Subordinated Debentures” and, together with the Series 2026A Junior Subordinated Debentures, the “Junior Subordinated Debentures”). The Company will pay interest on the Series 2026A Junior Subordinated Debentures (i) from and including the date of original issuance to, but not including, December 15, 2036, at an annual rate of 6.500% and (ii) from and including December 15, 2036, during each Interest Reset Period at an annual rate equal to the Five-Year Treasury Rate as of the most recent Reset Interest Determination Date plus 1.877%; provided, that the interest rate during any Interest Reset Period for the Series 2026A Junior Subordinated Debentures will not reset below 6.500% (which equals the initial interest rate on the Series 2026A Junior Subordinated Debentures). The Company will pay interest on the Series 2026B Junior Subordinated Debentures (i) from and including the date of original issuance to, but not including, December 15, 2033, at an annual rate of 6.500% and (ii) from and including December 15, 2033, during each Interest Reset Period at an annual rate equal to the Five-Year Treasury Rate as of the most recent Reset Interest Determination Date plus 2.030%; provided, that the interest rate during any Interest Reset Period for the Series 2026B Junior Subordinated Debentures will not reset below 6.500% (which equals the initial interest rate on the Series 2026B Junior Subordinated Debentures). 

 The sale of the Junior Subordinated Debentures closed on August 7, 2026. The Junior Subordinated Debentures were offered pursuant to the Company’s Registration Statement on Form S-3 (No. 333-289302), which became effective upon filing. The Junior Subordinated Debentures were issued pursuant to the Company’s Indenture (For Unsecured Subordinated Debt Securities), dated as of May 1, 2024, as heretofore supplemented, including by the Company’s Officer’s Certificate dated as of August 4, 2026, establishing the terms of the Series 2026A Junior Subordinated Debentures (the “Series 2026A Officer’s Certificate”), and Officer’s Certificate dated as of August 4, 2026, establishing the terms of the Series 2026B Junior Subordinated Debentures (the “Series 2026B Officer’s Certificate”). Capitalized terms used herein and not otherwise defined shall have the meanings given to them in the Series 2026A Officer’s Certificate and the Series 2026B Officer’s Certificate, as applicable, filed herewith as Exhibit 4(a) and Exhibit 4(b), respectively.
