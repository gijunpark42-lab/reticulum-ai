## Item 8.01
Item 8.01 Other Events.

Settlement of Underwritten Forward Sale Agreements

As previously disclosed, on March 17, 2025, Entergy Corporation (the “Company”) entered into underwritten forward sale agreements (the “2025 Underwritten Forward Sale Agreements”) with several counterparties relating to an aggregate of 17,796,401 shares of the Company common stock, par value $0.01 per share (the “Common Stock”).

On September 2, 2026, the Company physically settled its then outstanding obligations under the 2025 Underwritten Forward Sale Agreements by delivering an aggregate of 11,145,984 shares of Common Stock in exchange for total cash proceeds of approximately $913 million. After such settlement, the Company has no outstanding obligations under any of the 2025 Underwritten Forward Sale Agreements. 

The Company also has underwritten forward sale agreements outstanding that were executed by the Company with several counterparties on May 5, 2026, in connection with the registered underwritten offering of $2.175 billion relating to an aggregate of 19,247,788 shares of the Company Common Stock.
