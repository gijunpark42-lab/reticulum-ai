Flex ( FLEX 2.40% ) materially shifted its strategic trajectory by announcing the spin-off of its Cloud and Power Infrastructure segment into a new, stand-alone public company focused on AI-driven digital infrastructure. The CPI segment highlighted substantial revenue acceleration targets, underpinned by multi-year customer agreements—most notably with Google (NASDAQ: GOOGL)—and broadened market exposure beyond hyperscalers to colocation, neocloud, and utility clients. The company closed the acquisition of Electrical Power Products (EP2), strengthening its utility-grade capabilities for grid modernization and positioning to capture margin-accretive, long-cycle programs. Full-year and fourth-quarter financial results set new profit and margin records, with broad-based segment growth and disciplined capital deployment. Flex committed to elevated CapEx levels through fiscal 2027 to deliver current growth backlog and expects returns to normalized capital intensity for all segments by fiscal 2028.
