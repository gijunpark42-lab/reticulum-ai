[Jayshree Ullal — CEO]
FIRST $3 BILLION QUARTER (entire 2021 was $2.9B). AI-fabric momentum: Etherlink switches now exceed 100 CUMULATIVE CUSTOMERS (from 4-5 in 2024). New 7060X-E7: 100-terabit capacity, 1.6T throughput, and the FIRST LIQUID-COOLED options. SCALE-ACROSS: 7800 the flagship spine for distributed scale-across (traffic isolation, contextual routing, security); scale-across switching/routing TAM forecast ~$15-20 BILLION IN 2030; scale-across expected to be ~30% OF THE OVERALL AI TARGET OF AT LEAST $3.6 BILLION IN 2026. Supply chain: 'the industry is going to have a TWO-YEAR problem — I don't think we get out of it as an industry till 2028'; hired Eugenia Corrales (global ops exec); MULTI-YEAR PURCHASE COMMITMENTS NEARLY TRIPLED YoY from $3.6B to ~$9.7 BILLION. GUIDANCE RAISED FOR THE THIRD TIME THIS YEAR: 2026 revenue ~$12.6B = 40% GROWTH (+$2.1B over the $10.5B Analyst Day plan; +$1.1B over May's $11.5B) — spread across back-end AI fabrics, core front-end data center, campus, and routing.

[Ken Duda — President/CTO]
Three AI innovations: SSU (Smart System Upgrade — hitless software upgrades; no XPU downtime for security patching); MRC (Multipath Reliable Connection — senders SPRAY a single flow across many fabric paths, receivers reassemble out-of-order data, eliminating hash-collision slowdowns); SRv6 segment routing as AI-fabric load balancing (sender-tagged explicit paths + real-time congestion signaling), supported end-to-end from scale-out fabric to long-distance scale-across routing on one EOS binary.

[Todd Nightingale — COO]
Supply progress: multi-year agreements with strategic component vendors; new suppliers qualified; MEMORY SUPPLY SECURED FOR 2026 with extended visibility WELL INTO 2027 across DDR4/DDR5/NAND; PCB + optics capacity buildable in a 12-MONTH window; NEW LIQUID-COOLING SUPPLY CHAIN established (cold plates, quick disconnects, tubing with capacity agreements); three contract manufacturers + three distribution facilities (US/Asia/Mexico); better delivery terms, multi-sourcing, reduced inventory risk.

[Chantelle Breithaupt — CFO]
Q2 revenue $3.0B (+37.7%, vs $2.8B guided); international 23% of revenue. GM 63.4% (down YoY on customer mix; up QoQ on tariff refunds ~20-30bps + mix). Opex $411M = 13.5% of revenue (R&D $278M). OPERATING MARGIN 49.9% ($1.5B). Net income $1.3B (42.9%); EPS $1.02 (+39.7%). Cash $13.3B; NO buybacks in Q. OCF $1.1B. Inventory $2.5B (turns 1.7). PURCHASE COMMITMENTS $9.7B (from $8.9B Q1) — mostly chips for new products + AI. DEFERRED REVENUE $6.9B (product deferred +~$600M QoQ; acceptance-clause-heavy AI ramps). FY2026 raised to ~$12.6B (+40%): campus goal >= $1.25B, AI FABRICS GOAL >= $3.5B; FY GM 62-64% 'inclusive of anticipated supply-chain cost increases for MEMORY AND SILICON'; FY op margin RAISED to 48-49%. Q3 guide: ~$3.3B revenue, GM ~63%, op margin 48-49%, EPS $1.06-1.08. Visibility ~two quarters; 'guiding based on what we're CONFIDENT WE CAN GET THE SUPPLY FOR — if supply released more, there is opportunity to do better.'

[Q&A highlights]
- Amit Daryanani (Evercore): build-vs-buy shifting toward integrated platforms as AI networking complexity explodes; white box = tactical for simple scale-up/out; EOS system approach wins where job-completion time, reliability, MRC/SRv6/traffic engineering matter.
- David Vogt (UBS): the +$1.1B raise deliberately NOT allocated — AI and campus numbers 'absolutely' going up, flexibility retained to ship what supply allows; 'all numbers are going up.'
- Michael Ng (GS): supply — no magic wand; industry-wide tightness through ~2028; Arista's specific H1 actions pay off in H2 (silicon relationships, new vendor quals, shorter lead times, nimble SKU shifts).
- George Notter (Wolfe): CPO/NPO/XPO — scale-up philosophy 'COPPER IF YOU CAN, OPTICS IF YOU MUST'; against '5 different proprietary CPO implementations' — Arista committing to ONE OPEN CPO (socketed optical engines, pigtail fibers, fully pre-tested modules; often called NPO); trials/examples NEXT YEAR; 'majority of the world will still remain PLUGGABLE OPTICS AND COPPER; there will be SOME co-packaged optics in 2028 and 2029.'
- Ben Reitzes (Melius): AI labs — Anthropic webinar Sept 8 (network + model + security best-of-breed on vulnerabilities; 'the mythos and the vulnerabilities is very front and center'); network must be robust regardless of model (multi-model, training->inference).
- Aaron Rakers (WF): 10% customers — Microsoft and Meta remain committed partners; 'I fully expect there to be ONE, MAYBE TWO' additional 10% customers; flexibility retained.
- Karl Ackerman (BNP): scale-up — 'we live in an NVIDIA world': NVLink vertical stack = little Arista participation (Arista does better in scale-out/across); NON-NVIDIA accelerators (AMD MI series, Google TPUs — 'increasingly a FORMIDABLE training processor', in-house XPUs, inference accelerators) = Arista working BOTH scale-up and scale-out incl. CUSTOM RACKS tuned to their engines; 'feels like InfiniBand 2-3 years ago... it will take time to go from proprietary NVLink to alternatives, even if Ethernet's really good. I expect us to do much better there.'
- James Fish (Piper): 1.6T — history: 800G trials 2023-24, ramp 2025-26; 1.6T TRIALS H2 2026 with SINGLE-DIGIT but VERY LARGE customers; liquid-cooling infrastructure needed; REAL PRODUCTION 2027.
- Matt Niknam (Truist): GM bridge — tariff refunds ~20-30bps vs guide; rest customer mix; PRICE INCREASES only help 'end of the year or more like next year' (working through backlog).
- Simon Leopold (RJ): the raise spans ALL sectors — core front-end data center 'isn't going to be flat' (enterprise + new logos + international), plus routing adjacency (edge, DCI, scale-across).
- Jeff Hopson (Needham): scale-across is PERMANENT, not temporary — 'we're going to be in a CONSTANT STATE OF SCARCITY for power, space, and compute'; today dominated by cloud/AI titans, already extending to several NEOCLOUDS.
- Antoine Chkaiban (New Street): scale-across market ~$3-4B TODAY -> ~$15B(-20B incl optics) BY 2030; Arista capturing ~$1.2B in 2026.
- Meta Marshall (MS): 'blue box' (EOS) wins on 3 axes — operational excellence (neoclouds lack staff), AI feature depth (EOS + NetDI diagnostics), reliability/vulnerability; hybrid OpenNOS+EOS growing; pure white box persists for ultra-cheap or staff-rich titans.
- Adrienne (Citi): ~two quarters visibility; guide is supply-constrained-confident.
