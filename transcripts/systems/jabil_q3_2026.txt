=== HEADLINE RESULTS (Q3 FY2026) ===
- Net revenue: $8.75 billion, up ~12% YoY (vs $7.83B in Q3 FY2025).
- Core diluted EPS: $3.16 (beat consensus; GAAP diluted EPS $2.59).
- Core operating margin: 5.8%.
- Adjusted free cash flow: $359 million.
- Stock rose ~14% on the print.

=== SEGMENTS ===
- Intelligent Infrastructure: $4.2 billion, up 21% YoY; segment core operating margin 6.1%,
  up 80 bps YoY; driven by the AI data-center build-out. Cloud/data-center infrastructure
  growing double digits.
- Regulated Industries: ~$3.2 billion, up ~4% YoY.
- Connected Living & Digital Commerce: ~$1.4 billion, up ~5% YoY.

=== AI REVENUE & GUIDANCE ===
- FY2026 AI-related revenue outlook raised ~$500M from the March outlook to ~$13.6 billion,
  up ~50% YoY (from ~$9B in FY2025).
- FY2027: AI revenue expected to grow at a similar ~50% percentage rate off the larger base.
- FY2026 full-year: revenue ~$35B, core operating margin ~5.8%, core diluted EPS ~$12.70.
- Q4 FY2026 core operating margin guided to ~6.4% (midpoint).
- FY2027 core operating margin targeted above 6%. Full FY2027 guidance deferred to the
  September investor briefing.
- CapEx held at an asset-light 1.5%-2% of revenue despite significant capacity expansion.

=== CAPACITY EXPANSION ===
- "Expanding capacity in a disciplined way, tied to visible customer demand."
- North Carolina facility on track to complete in Q4 FY2026, full ramp expected by January 2027;
  one customer booked, seeking others. Run-rate expected in the "$1 billion, $2 billion,
  $3 billion range over the next one, two, and three years." Exploring adjacent land.
- Global footprint expanding by an incremental ~10% via new buildings and expansions.
- Memphis: expanding LV/MV switchgear and in-row heat exchangers (data-center power).
- Mexico: second hyperscaler work ramping.
- India: networking ramp nearly doubled revenue YoY.
- Existing footprint "should support the next stage of AI growth."

=== HYPERSCALER CUSTOMERS ===
- Three hyperscalers. First two already ramping.
- Third hyperscaler expected to contribute "a couple hundred million dollars in fiscal 2027,"
  potentially growing to ">$1 billion in fiscal 2028." Location undetermined (maybe NC).

=== NETWORKING & COMMUNICATIONS ===
- Up more than 50% YoY, supported by a strong networking ramp in India (revenue ~doubled YoY).
- InfiniBand and Ethernet demand cited as positive drivers.
- Customers investing in greater high-speed-interconnect capacity to keep pace with AI workloads.
- 5G spending showing signs of recovery.

=== SILICON PHOTONICS / OPTICS ===
- Silicon photonics an active participation area; margin-accretive driver within Intelligent
  Infrastructure. Builds on the photonics business acquired from Intel (co-packaged optics /
  near-package optics).

=== LIQUID COOLING & RACK INTEGRATION ===
- Next-gen liquid-cooled AI rack manufacturing capability highlighted.
- Rack-level integration combining compute, storage, networking, power distribution, advanced cooling.
- Hanley acquisition (modular power distribution, energy systems) integrating ahead of plan;
  contributes double-digit margins.

=== CAPITAL EQUIPMENT / TEST EQUIPMENT ===
- Capital equipment a high performer within Intelligent Infrastructure.
- Test-equipment demand "through the roof" due to rapid evolution of chip technology.
- Wafer-fab equipment (WFE) "making a little bit of a comeback" — demand environment improving.

=== SUPPLY-CHAIN CONSTRAINTS ===
- High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) in high demand; lead times extending.
- High-end, high-density interconnect PCBs in high demand.
- DDR4-and-below memory products face some level of shortages; DDR5 capacity "decent."
- Hyperscalers secure "more than their fair share" of constrained components.
- Mitigation: supply-chain strategy focused on access, allocation and long-term commitments
  rather than transactional pricing.

=== INDIA / ADANI PARTNERSHIP ===
- Strategic alliance with Adani Enterprises announced; "still early days," no definitive
  framework yet.
- Scope: multi-gigawatt manufacturing capacity for high-density AI racks — liquid-cooled AI
  racks, servers, storage systems, networking equipment, plus power distribution units,
  transformers, switchgear, thermal management. Target customers: hyperscalers, co-location
  providers, enterprise data centers.
- Fiscal 2028 is the realistic starting point for meaningful contributions.
