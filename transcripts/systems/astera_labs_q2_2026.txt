[Jitendra Mohan — CEO]
Since our last earnings call, investment in AI infrastructure has continued to accelerate. Multiple industry forecasts now point to hyperscaler AI and cloud infrastructure spending in 2027 reaching into the trillion-dollar range, with sovereign AI initiatives, inference workloads, and enterprise adoption expanding globally. Astera Labs delivered outstanding results in Q2, with record revenue of $392.4 million, up 27% sequentially and up 104% year-over-year. Broad-based strength across the entire product portfolio.

PCIe 6 momentum further accelerated in Q2, representing MORE THAN 50% of total revenue, driven by both Scorpio AI fabric switches and Aries retimers. On the AI fabric front, Scorpio delivered significant growth. High-radix Scorpio X-Series has entered VOLUME PRODUCTION and will continue to scale materially in the second half. We expect Scorpio to become our LARGEST PRODUCT FAMILY IN Q3, one quarter AHEAD of prior expectations. Shipping multiple configurations of Scorpio X-Series into scale-up applications to initial customers; on track to ship Scorpio X-Series to additional customers by year-end. Scorpio P-Series expanding across multiple customers (hyperscalers and AI infrastructure providers), currently pre-production, ramping more meaningfully in 2027. Upcoming AI systems will employ more complex switching topologies for larger XPU cluster sizes and higher bandwidth — expected to drive a MATERIAL INCREASE in silicon dollar content per XPU. Scorpio growth cycle: (1) higher dollar content per XPU (greater attach + capabilities), (2) expanding customer base, (3) greenfield TAM growing rapidly across training and inference.

Aries delivered RECORD quarterly revenue — retimers and gearboxes across scale-up and scale-out in AI and general-purpose compute. Growth from increased AI deployments, expanding cluster sizes, greater unit attach rates, higher ASPs on the PCIe 6 transition. Taurus grew strongly; shipped pre-production volume of 100G-per-lane Taurus smart cable modules for 800G AECs (scale-out). Expanded Taurus with a new family of 3.2T smart retimers and smart redrivers supporting 200G per lane for next-generation Ethernet and UALink — industry's first with SMART SWAP (choose low-power redriver or long-reach retimer without board redesign). New 200G/lane parts DOUBLE the Taurus market opportunity to OVER $4 BILLION BY 2030.

CXL: renewed momentum — for memory-intensive inference and agentic AI workloads, memory capacity/utilization are becoming system constraints and CXL-attached memory offers attractive cost-performance relative to local HBM. As hyperscalers address memory supply and pricing dynamics, additional opportunities in mainstream general-purpose servers. In Q2 closed a NEW DESIGN WIN with the standard Leo memory controller at a U.S. hyperscaler. Into 2027, expect to ship BOTH standard and custom Leo CXL memory controllers IN VOLUME across general-purpose compute and AI inferencing to TWO U.S. HYPERSCALERS.

[Sanjay Gajendra — President/COO]
Scorpio X-series addresses the $20 BILLION merchant scale-up switching TAM. Differentiation from the intelligence layer: hardware-accelerated HyperCast and in-network compute deliver up to 2x improvement in collective performance for large-scale training and inference. COSMOS software now extends beyond fleet management to dynamic traffic shaping and real-time performance management. Expect content opportunity for Scorpio X series ALONE to grow WELL BEYOND $1,000 PER XPU in future generations of AI platforms. Scorpio P long-term: modular AI servers, disaggregated KV cache appliances, storage, enterprise servers. Aries = gold standard across major XPU and CPU platforms for PCIe 5.0, continuing for 6.0; PCIe 7.0 solutions ready to meet customer timelines.

UALink: consortium 2.0 spec validates our architecture — codifies in-network compute, advanced RAS, and 200G SerDes as next-gen scale-up fabric requirements, directly aligned with our investments. On track to deliver UALINK-ENABLED SCORPIO X SERIES scale-up switches IN 2027.

Optical: substantial expansion vector — potential to unlock TENS OF BILLIONS of dollars beyond the copper TAM. Capabilities assembled across analog/mixed-signal, DSP, EIC/PIC integration, optical packaging (organic + aiXscale acquisition) → complete optical engines, not just components. Three phases: (1) high-density fiber-attached solutions (aiXscale core tech, up to 50-meter fiber, ultra-low latency) — initial opportunity with a TIER-1 AI PLATFORM PROVIDER expected to enter VOLUME PRODUCTION IN 2027; (2) near-packaged optics (NPO) chipsets for multi-rack scale-up clusters — also 2027 production; (3) fully integrated Scorpio X-Series fabric switches with CPO optical engine modules — 2028 AND BEYOND, terabits/sec optical I/O per switch.

Custom: executing across multiple vectors — custom solutions for hybrid rack architecture including NVLINK FUSION-BASED DESIGNS and, separately, custom Leo memory controllers in specialized interface applications. Initial shipments for these custom design wins commence 2027. Multi-billion dollar incremental opportunity over time. Platform philosophy: "AI Your Way" — standards-based or custom, copper or optical, scale-up or scale-out.
