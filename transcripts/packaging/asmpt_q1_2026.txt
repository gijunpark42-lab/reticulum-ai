=== PREPARED REMARKS (CEO Robin Ng) ===

This quarter ASMPT achieved the highest quarterly bookings and billings in the last few years. AI drives demand across multiple products as the rapid evolution of AI increases the value and complexity of back-end semiconductor manufacturing. New AI architectures demand heterogeneous integration, tighter interconnect pitch, higher bandwidth and power efficiency — driving higher precision, alignment and process-control needs across packaging flows, benefiting TCB, photonics, CPO, flip chip, and mainstream wire/die bonding and pick-and-place.

TCB — Logic: delivered sizable shipments for chip-to-substrate (C2S) applications, reinforcing leadership in C2S TCB. Received bookings for 4 ultra-fine-pitch chip-to-wafer (C2W) TCB tools featuring fluxless plasma-based AOR technology from a leading advanced logic customer. Actively engaging key logic players across multiple programs.

TCB — Memory: a key memory player is using a flux-based TCB tool for assembly and is also qualifying a fluxless AOR solution for HBM4 16-high.

Photonics: revenue grew nearly FIVEFOLD year-on-year on strong demand for 800G+ optical transceivers. The 1.6T transceiver solution received BULK ORDERS from leading optics suppliers in the data-center networking supply chain — market leader position.

CPO: a paradigm shift bringing optical engines closer to compute silicon. ASMPT CPO solutions enable high-precision bonding integrating fiber array unit (FAU), microlens, electronic IC and photonic IC into a single optical engine. Deepened engagement with multiple leading global players.

Flip chip: strong bookings growth QoQ and YoY from (1) accelerated 2.5D adoption for larger AI package sizes driving embedded-bridge die-bonding opportunities for chip-on-wafer AND chip-on-panel; (2) traction in panel-level fan-out for RF and power devices.

Mainstream: strong bookings for both SEMI and SMT. SEMI mainstream benefited from sustained utilization at leading IDMs/OSATs plus rising demand for AI DATA CENTER POWER MANAGEMENT solutions; China saw increased wire/die bonding demand. SMT achieved record bookings driven by AI servers, optical transceivers and China EVs; SMT high-flex high-force solutions for large-format boards are a leading choice for AI server assembly.

ASMPT received the Intel EPIC Supplier Award 2026 (highest supplier recognition).

=== CFO (Katie Xu) — Q1 2026 financials (continuing operations, non-HKFRS adjusted) ===

Group revenue USD 507.9M — flat QoQ, +32.0% YoY, above consensus, highest in 3 years. Group bookings USD 727.0M, +46.0% QoQ and +71.6% YoY — highest in 4 years (SMT products, wire bonders, die bonders, photonics). Adjusted gross margin 39.5%, +357bps QoQ (SEMI mix); -151bps YoY (higher SMT mix). Adjusted opex -4.6% QoQ, +12.4% YoY (FX + strategic infrastructure/R&D). Adjusted EPS HKD 0.81, +118.9% QoQ, +189.3% YoY, above consensus.

SEMI segment: revenue USD 274.5M (+12.2% QoQ, +14.6% YoY) — QoQ driven by high-end die bonders and TCB; YoY by AI applications. Bookings USD 309.6M (+22.6% QoQ, +43.2% YoY) — wire/die bonders from China OSATs, high-end smartphone, AI-related power management, optical transceivers. Book-to-bill 1.13 — third consecutive quarter of improvement. Adjusted GM 46.4% (+594bps QoQ, met guidance). Adjusted segment profit HKD 309.4M (+165.9% QoQ, +16.8% YoY).

SMT segment: revenue USD 233.5M (-11.0% QoQ seasonal, +60.7% YoY on AI servers + China EVs). RECORD bookings USD 417.4M (+70.0% QoQ, +101.1% YoY) — AI servers, optical transceivers, China EVs, robust China demand from global data-center expansion. Adjusted segment profit HKD 141.8M (-28.3% QoQ on volume, up YoY).

=== Q&A KEY POINTS ===

Memory TCB (Gokul/JPM): last memory TCB bulk order was Q4 2025; confident of orders when memory makers are ready. TCB TAM forecast of USD 1.6 BILLION (2028) intact; recent adjustments to next-gen GPU HBM4 roadmaps cause quarter-to-quarter variability in customer decision timing, but activity level very healthy. Target 35-40% market share of the whole TCB market (reiterated, "never wavered").

Biggest memory player (uses internal TCB tools): cannot name, but ASMPT is "in the process of finalizing an evaluation program with a key memory player" — a positive step toward enabling ASMPT technology as a memory process standard.

Logic CoW fluxless: 4 tools won in Q1 = start of the program; as GPU/ASIC architectures migrate from SoC to SoIC/chiplet configurations, more precision needed to place chips on interposer -> increasing TCB use. 2026 CoW tool count will NOT be significant; 2027 will be MEANINGFUL.

Photonics TAM: after a recent deep dive, combined optical-transceiver + CPO TAM "looks bigger than before" (not yet disclosed). Photonics revenue 5x YoY but from a small base (previously <$100M annual run-rate ballpark per analyst). Optical transceiver: moving 400G -> 800G -> 1.6T; ASMPT dominant, winning share; BOTH segments participate (SEMI tools for high-precision bonds, SMT pick-and-place for lower-precision components).

CPO applications (4 high-precision bonding areas): (1) FAU attach on PIC; (2) EIC stacking on PIC; (3) microlens attach on PIC; (4) whole optical engine onto substrate. Solutions for ALL of these; designed in with a number of key CPO players, serving BOTH GPU and ASIC makers. EIC-on-PIC could be hybrid bonding OR TCB. CPO equipment TAM becomes interesting from 2028 onwards. Possibly expanding beyond die bonding (e.g., AOI inspection for FAU alignment under study).

OSAT capex (Daisy/MS): strong wire-bond/die-bond demand from OSATs — driver is POWER APPLICATIONS going into data centers (new power devices -> new capacity); SMT record bookings largely AI server boards (many power packages). China: mainstream stronger than advanced packaging on SEMI side; China was ~41% of revenue last year; regional mix stays relatively stable with a bit more China OSAT bookings.

EMIB-T (Kevin/Citi): for embedded die bonding on the large ~510x500 panel substrate, ASMPT TCB is NOT ready yet (tool of that panel size takes time). BUT the customer already uses ASMPT CoW tools, and if EMIB-T proliferates they will need more tools to place components — ASMPT benefits on the CoW side.

CoPoS / chip-on-panel (Sunny/UBS): developing chip-on-panel (CoP) TCB tools NOW — demonstration tools to be delivered sometime this year (2026), engaged with the key advanced logic customer; panel starts at 310x310 form factor. TCB customer base/applications very diverse across logic + HBM.

SMT strategic review: still evaluating; "received some interest" in the SMT business. NEXX divestment: strategic decision (NEXX is middle-end deposition/plating, not back-end focus) — and yes, PCB/substrate-maker order pickup was visible. No timeline announced for SIPLACE/NEXX disposal.

Supply chain (Arthur/Macquarie): SMT revenue conversion is impacted by component supplier LEAD TIMES — expected to improve in H2 2026; SEMI supply tight but managed fine.
