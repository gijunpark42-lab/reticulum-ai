=== PREPARED REMARKS ===

CEO (Rafi Amit): Q1 revenue $121.7M, slightly ahead of guidance. Gross margin 51%; operating income $31M. ~50% of revenue AI-related products; additional ~20% from other advanced-packaging applications.

UNPRECEDENTED start to the year in incoming orders — significantly strengthens confidence for the remainder of 2026 and a strong foundation for 2027. Already received ORDERS AND FORECAST FROM 2 HBM MANUFACTURERS for 3D metrology and 2D inspection steps representing expected revenue EXCEEDING $260 MILLION FOR 2026 AND 2027. On top, significant incremental business expected from these 2 customers and other HPC players later this year and into 2027. Compelling opportunity in the OSAT domain undergoing a significant wave of AI-related advanced-packaging capacity investment — Camtek is the leading provider there for both 2D inspection and 3D metrology.

Q2 guidance $129-131M. SURGE expected in H2 2026: >25% HIGHER revenue vs H1, with potential additional upside based on timing of orders/deliveries between Q4 2026 and early 2027.

Strategy: maintain leadership share in 3D metrology while gaining share in 2D inspection — recent order wins demonstrate it. New systems Eagle G5 and Hawk (sub-6-micron bump-height metrology, inspection down to 100nm, higher throughput): together 30% of revenue last year, revenue from these platforms expected to DOUBLE in 2026. AI expertise + Visual Layer collaboration delivering breakthrough detection/metrology/classification (higher throughput, better sensitivity, fewer false alarms, better accuracy) — enthusiastic strategic-customer feedback. These innovations expected to expand 2D share and win additional process steps INCLUDING THE FRONT END — TAM expanding to OVER $2 BILLION IN 2027 (~+$0.5B vs ~$1.5-1.7B today). Visual Layer acquisition CLOSED a couple of weeks ago; full integration begun; plan to develop a dedicated AI-based software product line.

CFO (Moshe Eisenberg): Q1 revenue $121.7M (slightly above Q1-25). Gross profit $62M; GM 51% (similar to Q4) — GM to IMPROVE in H2 on volume + Hawk/G5 mix. Opex $30.9M (up on R&D, S&M, weaker USD/ILS); operating profit $31.1M; op margin 25.5% (vs 31.5% yr ago) — expected to RETURN TO ~30% in H2. Financial income $8.1M. Net income $35.3M, EPS $0.70 (vs $0.79). Cash & equivalents+deposits+securities $850M. Inventory optimized to $116.7M, will rise into the growth period. AR up to $131.7M on collection timing (lower cash generation this quarter). Q2 guide $129-131M.

=== Q&A KEY POINTS ===
- Visual Layer: unique annotation/classification technology; worked together over a year; acquisition brings full ownership, researchers join Camtek; combined with large in-house algorithm team. AI capabilities to be introduced to customers in the next few months; revenue contribution from these capabilities expected in H2 2026. Software monetization in 2 stages: (1) software/AI packages sold into the THOUSANDS-strong Camtek installed base; (2) broader AI software solutions for the semiconductor industry (Visual Layer expertise).
- China: positive trend, healthy, but 2026 growth mainly driven by NON-China markets. Competition: many small local players; only ONE meaningful local competitor; pressure at lower-end applications; very large installed base + OSAT buildup in China protects position.
- Lead times: Eagle ~3 months, Hawk 3-6 months; enough inventory/supply-chain flexibility to respond to upside; "no issues" supporting the $260M order/forecast.
- H2 +25% h/h growth drivers: advanced packaging lags front end by 1-2 quarters; unprecedented order flow; growth across applications/regions (OSATs, "1s and 2s"), AI the engine; growth starts already in Q2 and continues into 2027.
- US chiplet IDM customer (Intel implied): meaningful share there; business expected toward the LATTER PART of 2026 and into 2027; strong positions at both chiplet customers (also the Taiwan one).
- Photonics/optics: involved, opportunities exist but magnitude smaller than HBM/chiplet for now.
- Israel operations: no missed shipments despite the Middle East situation; capacity able to ramp; Germany capacity (~10%) expanding.
- Component/DRAM price increases: some supply-chain pressure but offset by ongoing cost reduction — H2 GM still expected to improve.
- HBM $260M order split: a significant number already ships in 2026, rest in 2027 ("big number coming this year").
