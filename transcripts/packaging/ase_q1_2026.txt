ATM (Assembly/Test/Materials = OSAT) segment:
- RECORD ATM revenue NTD 112.4B (+2% QoQ, +30% YoY). ATM gross margin 26% (ahead of 24.5% plan; -0.3pp QoQ, +3.4pp YoY). ATM operating margin 14.1% (+4.5pp YoY).
- ATM = 65% of consolidated revenue, 91% of consolidated operating profit (vs 58%/86% a year ago).
- Blended factory utilization ~80% (loading better; depressed by LEAP install + traditional consolidation).
- LEAP (leading-edge advanced packaging) used primarily in Computing applications; full-process LEAP lines in tuning/qualification, revenue weighted to Q4 2026.

EMS segment: revenue NTD 61.9B (-10% QoQ, -1% YoY); GM 9.5%; op margin 3.1%. Computing up on new AI accelerator products; Communications down on seasonality.

GUIDANCE / OUTLOOK:
- Raising 2026 CapEx: +NTD 0.9B buildings/infrastructure and +USD 0.6B machinery (mostly LEAP wafer sort, deployed Q4 for 2027 ramp). 2027 likely another CapEx-heavy year (funded by additional borrowing this year).
- 2026 LEAP services revenue raised ~10% above prior guidance to OVER USD 3.5B (more than doubling YoY); mainstream grows similar to last year (~13%). 2027: even stronger incremental LEAP revenue growth expected.
- LEAP composition: ~75% assembly / 25% test; within test ~75% wafer sort / 25% final test.
- Full-process LEAP (CoWoS-like): ~NTD 300M revenue mark this year, substantial growth in 2027; expanding customer base; should be accretive to LEAP margin in theory.
- CPO: working closely with foundry + end customer; will start with the packaging part then test; "a very, very critical partner once it gets into volume scale." Early stage, no number yet.
- Panel-level (large-reticle) packaging: fully-automated pilot line installed for customer qualification; small mass-production volume starting 2027; ASE would be first to ramp.
- General market: PC/smartphone softness continuing but more than offset by emerging AI-peripheral / AI-adjacent chips (power, connectivity, sensors, edge devices) plus automotive/industrial recovery → general-market growth ~13% (repeat of last year).
- Outside-Taiwan: main investment in Malaysia (acquired a factory from ADI = buffer capacity for non-Taiwan demand); Singapore ramping test capacity for AI-related test. Wafer-sort prioritized over final test (capacity/facility tight) incl. for a large GPU customer.
